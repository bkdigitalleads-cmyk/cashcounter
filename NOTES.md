## TestFlight checklist (1.0.0 build 1), around a real close

1. Fresh install: the three intro screens, "What are you counting?" (pick A shop till; the count should then say Front register), the trial and reminder screens, the paywall: weekly first and preselected, $4.99 with 3 days free, $19.99 yearly and $24.99 lifetime from the store, X after 3 seconds. Close it: "Counting is free." then the count, in your region's currency.
2. Count a mixed till: tap plus and minus on notes and coins with one thumb; check the subtotal on each row and the total at the bottom change at once and the figures do not jump sideways.
3. Type a large quantity: tap the number on Ones, type 347, then Next and keep going down the rows; Delete and Clear work; hold Delete to clear.
4. Add a roll: hold the Quarters row, Add a roll (40 coins, $10.00); hold Twenties, Add a strap (100 notes, $2,000.00), then Clear this row.
5. Set expected: tap Add expected amount, Float + sales, type a float and sales, Done. The total shows Short by or Over by in amber, or Balanced. Change one row until it balances.
6. Save: Save count, a name, Today, a note, Save count. The saved count opens; "Count saved." shows. Close and reopen the app: History has it under today.
7. Reopen: on the saved count tap Reopen, change a row, Save changes; History shows the new total, still one count. Duplicate: a new count with the same pieces.
8. Export the sheet: Count sheet, then Share (Messages or Mail), Print (Save as PDF from the print preview is fine), Save to Files; open the PDF in Files and check the rows, total, expected, difference, note and signature lines. "Count sheet ready." shows after each.
9. Deposit suggestion: on the saved count tap Deposit (opens the paywall on a free Apple ID; then try with a sandbox purchase); set a float; check it never banks more of a note than was counted, and leaves exactly the float when it can; Use as the usual float.
10. Hit both paywalls: on the free plan save a fourth count (paywall first, then saved, "History shows your last three."), export a second count's sheet (paywall), add a second till in Settings, Tills (paywall). The onboarding paywall again from the test menu (seven taps on the version line after a sandbox purchase), Show onboarding again.
11. Restore: on a second device or after deleting the app, Settings, Restore purchases, and from a paywall.
12. Settings: Rows on the count (hide pennies, show the $2 note, add Checks with Pro), Currency (change before the first save on a free Apple ID; after, it asks for Pro), Export history as CSV (open it in Numbers or Excel).
13. Dark mode at night, the largest Dynamic Type size, and VoiceOver on the count rows (each button says what it does: "One more, Twenties"), the keypad and the total.
14. The trial reminder notification a day before the sandbox trial ends; it is the only notification.

## Noticed, not changed

- The roll, bag and strap counts in src/currencies.ts are the usual bank wrappings as I know them; check them with a local bank before launch, especially the 50-note pound strap, the Australian rolls and the US half-dollar and dollar-coin rolls. Mexico has no coin roll offered, since I could not settle on one count used everywhere.
- The Spanish keywords include "caja", which is already in the Spanish subtitle, so it adds nothing there; swapping it frees 4 characters. Kept as briefed.
- The rating ask fires once ever (the engine's rule), on the first later-day save. The brief says "at most one per launch"; once ever is stricter. Say if it should ask again on later launches.
- On the free plan the paywall opens on every save after the third, not only the fourth. Each save still goes through.
- "Extra currencies" is read as: the free plan counts in one currency, changeable until the first saved count; after that another currency is Pro. Each till can have its own currency with Pro.
- The hidden test menu shows in test builds only, which the engine defines as development builds or a phone whose Pro is a sandbox purchase. On a TestFlight build with no sandbox purchase yet it does not appear; buy the sandbox weekly first.
- History's till filter shows All plus the first three tills that have counts.
- The count in progress is written 300 ms after the last tap; force-quitting inside that moment can lose the last tap.
- The CSV is UTF-8 without a byte order mark; older Excel versions may show "€" or "£" wrongly in the header. Numbers and Google Sheets read it fine.
- The renders come from a react-native-web copy; the iOS switch, the keyboard, long-press timing and the share and print sheets look or behave slightly differently on a phone.
- The on-screen count sheet is drawn by react-native-svg with the registered Barlow Tab names; only a device build shows whether its text sits exactly where pdf-lib puts it (both use the same font files and widths).
