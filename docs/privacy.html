Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy Cash Counter Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your tills, counts, notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and nothing you type is uploaded. A count sheet or a CSV file leaves your phone only when you share, print or save it.

What the app does not use

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. The app asks for one permission, notifications, only to remind you the day before a free trial ends, and only if you start one.
